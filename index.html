The fourteen
giants

Only fourteen mountains on Earth rise above 8,000 metres. Ten stand in the Himalaya and four in the neighbouring Karakoram. Tap a summit to meet it.

Heights are drawn to scale, but the chart starts at 7,700 m.

The death zone

Above about 8,000 m the air holds roughly a third of the pressure found at sea level. The body cannot adapt, so climbers move fast and carry oxygen. Every peak here crosses the dashed line.

First on top

Annapurna I was the first of the fourteen to be climbed, in 1950. Shishapangma was the last, in 1964.

Winter is different

K2 was the last eight-thousander climbed in winter. A team of Nepali climbers reached its summit in January 2021.

Heights are widely published figures; Everest uses the 2020 Nepal–China survey value. Air pressure is an approximation based on altitude.
